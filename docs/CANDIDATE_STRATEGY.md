## SECTION 1: IDENTITY NARRATIVE

Vibhor's career follows a single diagnostic arc: each transition happened when the current instrument hit its ceiling.

Constitutional litigation in Delhi showed how legal systems allocate rights, and how comprehensively they fail to deliver them at scale. Public sector consulting at IPAC showed how policy shapes systems, and how well-designed interventions die between design and delivery. Digital product work at government scale (3M+ users, 15,004 delivery centres) showed what it takes to reach people, and how fragile that reach is when governance is an afterthought. The MPP at Hertie was the analytical consolidation: "From Guarantee to Guardrail", a 15-indicator framework measuring how AI governance operationalises constitutional equality, across EU and Indian regulatory contexts. Gyro Health is the convergence: a care companion for people with long-term conditions, built solo with AI coding agents, with compliance as architecture, decisions and pass thresholds written before any data, and an engine held from release until measurement said it would not mislead the people it was built for.

The critical framing: this is not a series of career phases. It is one continuous problem-solving methodology applied across different mediums. Appellate litigation treats complex rules-based systems as adversarial architecture problems. IPAC is population-scale systems engineering. Gyro Health is ML architecture, product delivery, and regulatory compliance designed as integrated constraints. The instrument changed each time. The method did not.

Gyro Health is not the end of the arc. It is the first instance of the thing the whole method has been converging on: an individual holds a legitimate entitlement, faces an institution with the power to refuse, and loses because they cannot assemble proof. Gyro Health builds that proof for a patient facing a clinician. Haqdaar started as the same thing for a gig worker facing a platform (v1, April 2026) and is now narrowing to cheque bounce and tenancy disputes, where the remedy already exists at Lok Adalats and what the citizen lacks is the route to it. Same product category, different gatekeeper: the missing piece is sometimes the proof and sometimes the path, and both products build whichever one is missing.

**Writing rule that follows from this:** state the continuity positively. Never name what it is not. See Section 4, Objection 6.

**Deployment note.** The full arc including the mechanism is the founder narrative and belongs in public writing. Inside an application, use as much of it as the role rewards. For Track D and Track A, stop at Gyro Health and let the mechanism stay implicit. For Track E, restate the method in user terms (find where the people using a system drop out, then rebuild around what they actually do) and keep the legal and policy layers out of the opening, unless the product's users are lawyers or another group he has belonged to (APPLICATION_RULES.md Section 2, insider-user variant); the mechanism stays implicit. For Track C and Track B, the mechanism is the strongest part of the story and should be stated.

## SECTION 1A: THE EMPLOYMENT THESIS

Employment is primary for the next two years. Neither Gyro Health nor Haqdaar has shipped, neither generates income, and a role now is what funds the runway and closes a real capability gap.

**The gap a job closes.** He has built products from zero, alone. He has never built inside an established product organisation with a platform, an install base, a design system, a data team and a release process. That is a genuine gap, it is not closeable by founding another company, and it is the single strongest honest reason he wants the role. Use it. It is also the limitation confession in Paragraph 2 of most letters, where it must name the specific thing this employer has (a named user base, founder proximity, ownership scope, a product surface), never scale in general (APPLICATION_RULES.md Section 2).

**What a role must give.**
1. Product craft at scale inside an established organisation. This is the one requirement.
2. Income sufficient to keep the products alive on evenings and weekends.

**What strengthens a role, without being required.**
- A regulated or institutionally constrained domain, so the regulatory layer stays live rather than decaying.
- Something that compounds with building digital public and social infrastructure later: delivery at population scale, public sector or institutional customers, digital public infrastructure, health systems, or AI governance in production.

**Consumer and general PM roles.** A consumer or generalist PM role at a company with real scale meets the one requirement more directly than many regulated roles, because the install base, the experimentation culture, the data team and the release cadence are the craft. It is a legitimate target (Track E), not a fallback and not an out-of-track stretch. Do not score it down for lacking a regulated domain.

**What disqualifies a role regardless of fit.** See Section 2, exclusions. In short: direct Gyro Health competitors, defence and weapons, mass surveillance, and anything he would have to keep off his own public feed.

**One question to raise with every serious employer before accepting, not after.** Leave allowance, and whether remote work from abroad is genuinely used by the team rather than nominally permitted. The founder strategy's India ground loop, Section 8, depends on a fixed annual block of real time in India, and that is a leave-policy question, not an aspiration. Ask it as an ordinary logistics question in a later-stage conversation, not as a condition attached to the offer.

**How this interacts with the public profile.** The profile is founder-positioned. That lowers conversion on some Track D and Track E roles and that cost is accepted deliberately. The compensating move is that the cover letter and CV do the track-specific work, and the founder profile answers the question a hiring manager silently asks about a candidate with a non-linear background: is this person actually building things, or just describing them.

### Exclusion rules, all tracks

Screened before suitability assessment. A role hitting any of these is declined regardless of fit score.

1. **Direct Gyro Health competitor.** Patient-facing episode, symptom or medicine logging, or between-visit self-management, for epilepsy, migraine or any other long-term condition; AI symptom checkers aimed at patients. **The test:** is the product's core loop a patient logging episodes, symptoms or medicines, or managing a long-term condition between visits, or a patient-facing symptom checker? If yes, it is a competitor. If no, it is in scope. **Explicitly in scope:** fitness, nutrition, sleep, meditation and general wellness apps, and all adjacent health tech: clinical workflow, provider software, health infrastructure, payer systems, medtech, diagnostics, pharma digital, health data platforms. **Judgment call, raised with Vibhor per role:** reproductive and menstrual tracking apps (no longer an automatic decline).

3. **Mass surveillance and predictive policing products.** Same reason.
4. **Anything he would have to keep off his own public feed.** If taking the role would require the writing to go quiet or go anonymous, the role costs more than it pays.

**Judgment call, not a rule:** Palantir and similar dual-use government platforms. Genuine product craft, genuine institutional access, and a real cost to the public position. Decide consciously per role, log the reasoning, do not apply by default.

## SECTION 4: KNOWN OBJECTIONS AND BRIDGES

### Objection 1: non-linear PM path, no prior PM title
**Bridge:** non-linear on title, linear on skill accumulation. Legal research is requirement decomposition under adversarial conditions. IPAC is PM work without the title: project lead on a failing 3M user platform that the team rebuilt around one measurable outcome, with post-launch quality monitoring owned. Gyro Health is full technical PM ownership with the zero-to-MVP arc. Title gap, not capability gap.
**Deploy:** CV framing and Para 2. Frame IPAC in PM language so the objection never gets raised.

### Objection 2: Indian national in Berlin, work authorisation
**Bridge:** Berlin residence permit, right to work in Germany, no sponsorship required. State in outreach only, unless context makes it obviously relevant. For EMEA-facing enterprise roles, fluency across Indian and EU regulatory and delivery contexts is a genuine differentiator.

### Objection 3: startup founder, pre-seed, no product shipped at scale
**Bridge:** Gyro Health shows he builds while thinking and has real skin in the domain. Then: "a founder's perspective on product decisions without the founder's ego about who makes the call." Founder experience plus IPAC delivery at scale argues he can operate inside an existing structure while bringing ownership instincts.

### Objection 4: German at A2
**Bridge:** calibration constraint, not a universal objection. Irrelevant for English-first roles. State A2 developing towards B1 where asked, never overclaim. Do not apply to hard German requirement roles unless the rest of the profile is strong enough to justify the exception.

### Objection 5: policy background without private-sector PM experience (Track B)
**Bridge:** Haqdaar is a production system on a public API, solo built. Gyro Health is full product ownership including architecture, research, compliance, and the evidence chain, built solo with AI coding agents. The advisory practice turns cross-border data protection obligations into practical requirements. The policy training sits on top of delivery experience, not in place of it.

### Objection 6: the pivot narrative (Track D, the critical one)

**This objection is pre-empted by framing, never answered.** Do not use a sentence that names the objection. "My background is not a pivot from law to technology" is banned in all outputs: it plants the doubt it was meant to remove, and it reads as defensive.

**Use the positive continuity form.** Approved openings for Para 2:
- "The same method runs through all of it. Take a complex rule-bound system, find where it fails under pressure, rebuild the requirements from there."
- "Every job I have held has been the same job in a different medium: work out why a rule-bound system is failing the people inside it, then rebuild it so it does not."
- "Appellate work, government delivery, and building Gyro Health have been one continuous exercise in the same skill at different scales."

**Then carry it through the specifics:**

## SECTION 10: PORTFOLIO USAGE

The portfolio (vibhor.work, the only domain used in applications; the old producthq.workers.dev address is retired) is linked from every CV, so every application is now checkable against it in one click. PORTFOLIO_DOSSIER.md is the readable record of the site, since the site itself renders client-side and cannot be fetched as text.

**What each surface does.** The CV carries outcomes. The portfolio carries judgment: the decision, the tradeoff accepted, and what was measured versus projected. A portfolio reference in an application earns its place only if it adds that judgment layer. Restating a CV bullet with a link attached is a defect.

**Selection.** Start from the track routing table in PORTFOLIO_DOSSIER.md, then pick the entry that best proves the JD responsibility Paragraph 3 opens on. One lead entry per application. A second entry only in outreach, never in the letter.

**In the cover letter.** One entry, in Paragraph 3, as a decision story (the choice, the alternative rejected, and why) serving as evidence for a named JD responsibility. One sentence, two at most, with the entry's deep link written once in plain text. It counts against the Para 3 budget. It never displaces the founder disclosure, the limitation confession or the forward move. In the evidence-table format it sits in the follow-up sentence after the table, never in a cell.

**Ventures.** The site shows Gyro Health, RasaHub and Haqdaar, the last two labelled side quests. The founder disclosure in the letter covers Gyro Health only. RasaHub and Haqdaar are cited as case studies of a decision, never as companies he runs, and never alongside the Gyro Health disclosure in the same sentence.

**Precedence.** On facts, the CVs, Section 5A and CURO_UPDATE_2026-10-21.md Section 2 govern. The dossier governs how pre-launch work is described.

**Live-site figures.** Until the WEBSITE_CHANGES items for the CMGRS 28% and the BeamerHeld 109 residents are live, the site still shows the retired figures. Applications use the corrected figures, and never quote the figure in the same sentence as that page's deep link.
