In appellate litigation, that meant isolating procedural edge cases under time pressure. At IPAC, I was project lead and owned post-launch quality monitoring on a 3M+ user grievance platform; the team rebuilt its requirements, and average days to case closure fell 28%. Gyro Health, the health app I founded, is early stage and pre-revenue, and I have built it myself with AI coding agents. Its safeguards have only been tested in simulation. In this role I'd run each Northwind Shield finding as a case only a retest can close. Two error rates, both measured, and a bar nobody loosens to hit a launch date. Northwind Labs puts “safety users never have to think about” at the core, and building that quiet safety while keeping lawful requests answered is the work I want next.
